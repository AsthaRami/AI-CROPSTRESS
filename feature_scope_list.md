# Project Feature Scope List: AI-Based Early Crop Stress Detection

This document outlines the current functional capabilities and future development directions for the AI-CropStress ecosystem.

---

## 1. Core AI Engine Capabilities
*   **Deep Learning Scanner**: Proprietary CNN architecture for pixel-level identification of 38+ crop stress types (fungal, viral, bacterial, and nutrient-based).
*   **Multi-Crop Diagnostic Support**: Specialized models for Tomato, Potato, Corn, Wheat, Cotton, and Rice.
*   **Preliminary AI Diagnosis**: Instant analysis provided immediately after image upload to offer immediate peace of mind or intervention steps.
*   **Regional AI Kisan Bot**: A personalized AI agriculture assistant trained on regional datasets, supporting voice/text in English, Hindi, and Gujarati.

---

## 2. Farmer Ecosystem (Portal & Dashboard)
*   **Precision Dashboard**: Real-time stats showing Healthy vs. Stressed scans, active alerts, and farm intelligence metrics.
*   **Smart Farm Management**: Digital twin creation for multiple farms, including data on soil type, acreage, and geographical coordinates.
*   **Automated Health Reports**: Visual generation of crop health reports (via html2canvas) for record-keeping or shared diagnostics.
*   **Expert Consultation ("Ask Expert")**: Direct bridge to human agronomists. Farmers can upload queries with images for personalized professional advice.
*   **Real-time Intelligence**: 
    *   **Weather Pulse**: Localized weather monitoring and agricultural recommendations based on humidity/temp.
    *   **Market Monitoring**: Integration of Mandi (Market) prices for local crops.
*   **Notification Center**: Critical alerts for nearby stress outbreaks (within 5km radius) and status updates on expert queries.

---

## 3. Agronomist / Expert Portal
*   **Specialist Queue**: Unified dashboard for reviewing pending diagnostic requests from the farmer community.
*   **Diagnostic Tools**: Side-by-side comparison of AI suggestions vs. expert analysis for high-accuracy resolution.
*   **Treatment Protocol Submission**: Formal interface to provide validated medicine names, dosages, and biological control steps.
*   **Professional Profiles**: Dedicated accounts for agronomists to manage their credentials and resolution history.

---

## 4. Modern UX & Branding
*   **Premium Multilingualism**: Full platform translation into English, Hindi, and Gujarati to bridge the digital divide.
*   **Adaptive Theme Engine**: High-contrast Dark Mode and Sleek Light Mode for varying field lighting conditions.
*   **Mobile-First Design**: Fully responsive layout optimized for varied mobile hardware common in rural environments.
*   **Glassmorphism UI**: High-end landing page and dashboard visuals to build trust and professional appeal.

---

## 5. Future Strategic Scope
*   **IoT & Sensor Fusion**: Real-time synchronization with on-field soil moisture, NPK, and temperature sensors.
*   **Satellite Vegetation Indices**: Integration of NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) for macro-level farm monitoring via satellite data.
*   **Community Watch (Social Network)**: A peer-to-peer warning system for farmers to highlight pest migrations or disease spreads in real-time.
*   **Agri-Marketplace Integration**: Direct links to purchase recommended bio-pesticides and certified seeds within the app.
*   **Offline Mode (PWA)**: Enabling AI diagnostics in remote field locations with poor or no internet connectivity.
*   **Yield Prediction**: Using historical health data and weather patterns to predict harvest quality and quantity.
